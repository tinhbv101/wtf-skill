# Reality numbers to crush the delusion

Checked in **September 2026**. Every block cites its source and as-of date. When speaking, round to orders of magnitude ("nearly 80,000", "over 3 billion") - numbers shift every quarter and rounding keeps you right. Lines marked *(order of magnitude)* weren't re-verified; never make them more precise. For products not listed: look them up with a source if web search is available, otherwise stay with cautious orders of magnitude.

## Social networks and messaging

**Meta / Facebook**
- Founded 2004 - 20+ years of operations
- 78,865 employees at 31 Dec 2025 (say "~79,000")
- Facebook: 3.07 billion monthly users in Dec 2023, the last Facebook-only figure Meta published (it now reports combined daily users across its apps). Say "over 3 billion"
- ~15,000 content reviewers inside ~40,000 safety and security staff (stated to the US Senate, 2024)
- Source: Meta 10-K FY2025 (sec.gov); TechCrunch 26 Jul 2023; Zuckerberg QFR responses, US Senate Judiciary, 31 Jan 2024

**Instagram** (the "tiny team" example people misuse)
- Launched Oct 2010; Facebook announced the $1B acquisition on 9 Apr 2012, with 13 employees - about 18 months after launch
- Then scaled on Facebook's infrastructure
- Source: CNN Money, 9 Apr 2012

**WhatsApp** (another "tiny team" example)
- Founded 2009; acquisition announced 19 Feb 2014 for ~$16B (~$19B including $3B of restricted stock for staff), with 450M+ monthly users
- ~55 employees at the time *(order of magnitude - aggregator sources only)*
- The founders had spent years as Yahoo engineers, and chose Erlang, a language built for telecom-grade messaging
- Roast angle: small teams build big products over years with deep expertise, not in one hour of prompting
- Source: Facebook press release, 19 Feb 2014

**X / Twitter**
- Roughly 7,500 staff when Musk bought it in Oct 2022; about 80% were let go, leaving ~1,500
- Roast angle: the most aggressive headcount cut in big tech still left ~1,500 people keeping the lights on. Your plan is one person and a chatbot
- Early years were famous for the "Fail Whale" outages under load *(qualitative, widely reported)*
- Source: CNN, 12 Apr 2023 (Musk interview with the BBC)

**Zalo (VNG)**
- 79.2 million monthly active users in Q3 2025; ~2.1 billion messages per day
- More than 17,000 government and public-service accounts on Zalo by end of 2024
- VNG had 3,327 employees at end of 2025
- Source: Tuoi Tre News, 29 Dec 2025; Tuoi Tre, 10 Feb 2026

## Ride-hailing and delivery

**Uber**
- Founded 2009
- ~34,000 employees at end of 2025, operating in 70+ countries and 15,000+ cities, each with its own rules and ways of paying
- Under the waterline: live dispatch, surge pricing, mapping, driver checks, insurance, incident response, fraud, litigation
- Source: Uber 10-K FY2025 (sec.gov)

**Grab**
- Founded 2012 (as MyTeksi, Malaysia)
- 12,012 employees at end of 2025
- Rides, food delivery, payments and lending in one app - with separate financial licenses country by country
- Source: Grab 20-F FY2025 via stockanalysis.com

**Gojek** (a big, funded player that walked away)
- Announced on 5 Sep 2024 it would stop operating in Vietnam from 16 Sep 2024
- Roast angle: an experienced regional giant with deep pockets left Vietnam. A weekend build is not going to take its place
- Source: VnEconomy, 5 Sep 2024

**Be (Be Group)**
- Founded 2018
- Company-reported: ~500,000 driver partners, 14 million users, 200,000+ merchant partners; an independent survey (Q&Me, Dec 2025 - Jan 2026) put its ride-hailing share at ~4%
- Roast angle: even with half a million drivers, a local player fights for single-digit share
- Source: be.com.vn, 16 Nov 2020; VietnamFinance, Feb 2026

**Xanh SM (GSM, Vingroup ecosystem)**
- Started operating on 14 Apr 2023 in Hanoi, with an all-electric fleet
- 30,000+ e-taxis by May 2024
- Roast angle: it entered the market with its own fleet of tens of thousands of cars and a conglomerate behind it - not with an app
- Source: Wikipedia "Xanh SM" (medium confidence; use orders of magnitude)

## E-commerce and marketplaces

**Amazon**
- Founded 1994, 30+ years
- ~1,576,000 full- and part-time employees at 31 Dec 2025 (say "over 1.5 million")
- Source: Amazon 10-K FY2025 (sec.gov)

**Shopify**
- Founded 2006 (grew out of an online snowboard shop)
- ~7,600 employees at end of 2025; GMV of $378.4 billion in 2025; "millions" of merchants in 175+ countries
- Source: Shopify 10-K FY2025 (sec.gov)

**Shopee / Sea Group**
- Shopee launched 2015; Sea has tens of thousands of staff *(order of magnitude, not re-verified)*
- Under the waterline: fulfilment and warehouses, payments, fake-goods policing, flash-sale traffic spikes, support, returns
- Source: not re-verified in Sep 2026 - keep to orders of magnitude

**Tiki** (the "local e-commerce is brutal" example)
- Founded 2010
- Market share fell from ~17% (2018) to under 1% (late 2024); Tiki Global lost ~US$83 million in the fiscal year to 31 Mar 2023; the founder stepped down as CEO in Jul 2023
- Roast angle: a well-funded local champion with VNG and JD.com as shareholders got squeezed out - by competitors, not by slow coding
- Source: VietnamBiz, 27 Feb 2025; Dan Tri, 13 Jul 2023

**Airbnb**
- Founded Aug 2008; the founders sold novelty cereal boxes ("Obama O's") in 2008 to raise ~$30K and stay alive
- ~8,200 employees plus ~13,000 third-party support workers at end of 2025
- Roast angle: support alone needs more people than most startups will ever hire
- Source: Airbnb 10-K FY2025 (sec.gov); Yahoo Finance / Benzinga

## Payments and fintech

**Stripe**
- Founded 2010
- $1.9 trillion total payment volume in 2025; 5+ million businesses
- Licensed money transmitter in ~53 US jurisdictions, plus an EU e-money license - licenses are a product in themselves
- Headcount: ~8,500 in early 2025 *(medium confidence)*
- Source: Stripe 2025 annual letter; stripe.com/legal/spc/licenses (read Sep 2026); Payments Dive, Jan 2025

**MoMo (M_Service)**
- Launched 2010; e-wallet license No. 16/GP-NHNN from the State Bank of Vietnam on 16 Oct 2015 - five years after launch
- 31+ million users; nearly 2,000 staff including 1,000+ engineers and 200+ AI engineers
- Roast angle: the country's biggest e-wallet has a thousand engineers and a central-bank license. You have a Supabase free tier
- Source: momo.careers/about (read Sep 2026); MoMo press release, Oct 2015

**VNPay**
- 40+ partner banks, 600,000+ payment acceptance points, 50+ million users
- Source: vnpay.vn (read Sep 2026, company-reported)

## SaaS and productivity

**Notion**
- Founded 2013; in 2015 the team was down to ~4 people, nearly out of money, and moved to Kyoto to rewrite the product from scratch
- Real traction only after Notion 2.0 in 2018; 100 million users in Aug 2024
- Roast angle: ~5 years and several rebuilds before it took off - with world-class designers
- Source: Figma blog, 6 Mar 2019; Notion blog, 3 Sep 2024

**Slack**
- Came out of Tiny Speck (founded 2009), whose game Glitch failed; Slack launched publicly in Feb 2014
- Salesforce completed the $27.7 billion acquisition on 21 Jul 2021
- Source: Salesforce press release, 21 Jul 2021; Wikipedia "Slack Technologies"

**Figma**
- Founded Oct 2012; invite-only preview Dec 2015; public release Sep 2016 - nearly four years building real-time multiplayer in the browser before launch
- IPO on the NYSE in Jul 2025; 1,886 full-time employees at end of 2025
- Source: Figma 10-K FY2025 (sec.gov); CNBC, Jul 2025; Wikipedia "Figma"

**MISA** (the Vietnamese accounting-software example)
- Founded 1994 - 30+ years
- Company-reported: 400,000 businesses and household businesses, 45,000 public-sector units, 3.5 million individuals, 20,000+ customers abroad in 22 countries
- ~4,200 employees (2025) *(medium confidence - Wikipedia only)*
- Under the waterline: separate product lines for enterprises, public sector and schools, each under a different accounting regime that changes by law (see `waterline.md`, Vietnam)
- Source: misa.vn/cong-ty (read Sep 2026); Wikipedia "Công ty MISA"

## AI

**OpenAI / ChatGPT**
- 1.2+ billion weekly ChatGPT users (announced 29 Sep 2026; 900 million in Feb 2026)
- ~4,500 employees in Mar 2026, planning ~8,000 by end of 2026
- Compute: Stargate - $500 billion over 4 years with Oracle and SoftBank; OpenAI later targeted ~$600 billion of compute spend by 2030
- Roast angle: your "ChatGPT competitor" is a text box calling their API - the company behind it is spending hundreds of billions on compute
- Source: The Decoder, 29 Sep 2026; Search Engine Land, Feb 2026; Fortune (FT), 21 Mar 2026; OpenAI "Announcing the Stargate Project"; CNBC, 20 Feb 2026

**Frontier model training cost**
- Asked whether GPT-4 cost $100 million to train, Sam Altman said "it's more than that" (Apr 2023)
- Epoch AI estimates frontier training-run costs grow ~2.4x per year, passing $1 billion around 2027
- Source: MIT event, Apr 2023 (reported); Epoch AI, "How much does it cost to train frontier AI models?"

## General patterns

These are qualitative - use them without attaching made-up statistics.

- Writing the code is a minority of the work behind any product that lasts; the rest is users, operations, post-launch fixes, support, compliance and marketing
- Startups usually fail from building something nobody wants, rarely from typing too slowly
- You can copy the code of a network; you can't copy the people on it
- Every super app began as one thing done well - Grab with taxis, Shopee with a marketplace, Zalo with chat - and grew from there
- "Tiny team" success stories (Instagram, WhatsApp, Notion) all took years and were built by unusually experienced people
